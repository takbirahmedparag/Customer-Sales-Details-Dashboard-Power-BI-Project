# Customer-Sales-Details-Dashboard-Power-BI-Project
This is my Power BI dashboard where I analyzed customer sales behavior using attributes like gender, occupation, marital status, and home ownership. I transformed data, built relationships, and created multiple measures to understand customer value and trends.

## Project Objective
The main goal of this project is to understand customer behavior and identify high-value customers based on their purchasing patterns and demographics.

## Key Insights & Analysis
In this dashboard, I have created:
•	Total Customers overview
•	Revenue per Customer analysis
•	Orders per Customer
•	High Value Customer identification
•	Customer Trend over Time
•	Sales by Occupation
•	Gender-wise and House Owner-wise distribution
•	Top Customer List

Measures & KPIs
Measure Name	                Description
Total Customer	              Total number of customers
Total Sales	                  Overall sales generated
Revenue per Customer	        Average revenue generated per customer
Orders per Customer	          Average number of orders per customer
High Value Customer	          Customers with high purchase value
% Male Customer	              Percentage of male customers
% Female Customer	            Percentage of female customers
House Owner Customer %	      Percentage of customers who own a house


## Features
•	Interactive Slicers (Gender, Occupation, Marital Status, Home Owner, Year)
•	KPI Cards (Total Customers, Revenue per Customer, Orders per Customer, High Value Customers)
•	Donut Charts (Gender Split, Home Owner Split)
•	Bar Chart (Sales by Occupation)
•	Line Chart (Customer Trend Over Time)
•	Table (Top Customers List

## Story & What I Solved
In this project, I worked deeply on customer-level analysis and solved multiple real problems step by step.
At first, I faced confusion while creating measures like Revenue per Customer and Orders per Customer, but later I understood how to calculate them using total values divided by customer count.
I also created percentage-based measures like % Male Customer, % Female Customer, and House Owner Customer %, which helped me understand customer distribution clearly.
One of the important parts was identifying High Value Customers, which improved my understanding of customer segmentation.
Managing multiple slicers (Gender, Occupation, Marital Status, Home Owner, Year) was also challenging. I learned how filters interact with visuals and make dashboards dynamic.
I also improved my understanding of visual selection, like when to use donut charts for distribution, bar charts for comparison, and line charts for trends
